The Sheetz menu: a complete 2026 guide

Sheetz started as a convenience store and grew into one of the most loved road-food menus in the eastern U.S. Here's how the Sheetz menu works, what each section costs and how to get the most out of it.

How the Sheetz menu works: MTO

Most of the Sheetz menu is MTO, made to order. You order on the touchscreen kiosk in the store or in the Sheetz app, choose your base (bun, roll, wrap, bowl or salad), then your protein, cheese, toppings and sauce. Many add-ons cost nothing extra, which is why fans build their own "secret menu" creations. Your order number is called when it's ready, and most stores keep the kitchen open 24/7. Every MTO base and topping is listed on the Sheetz MTO Menu page.

Burgers, subs and chicken

The Classic Burger starts at about $3.99 for a single. The new Hot Spicy Chicken Burger is around $5.99 and the Plant-Based MTO Burger around $6.49. Six-inch subs such as the Meatball Sub ($4.99) and Turkey Sub ($5.49) are popular lunches. MTO Grilled, Homestyle or Spicy Chicken sandwiches cost about $4.99 each. For a light bite, the Shnack Wrap is only about $2.49.

Bowls and salads

The new Southwest Steak Bowl is around $8.99. Made To Order Salads start at about $8.49, the Low Calorie MTO Salad is around $8.39, Crispy Chicken and Premium Chicken salads are about $10.79, and the Brisket salad tops the range at about $13.19.

Fryz, sides and appetizers

Sheetz is famous for its sides. Mac 'n Cheese Bites (5 pieces) are about $2.99. Larger shareable orders include Mozzarella Sticks ($9.39), Jalapeño Fryz ($9.99) and Wisconsin Cheese Bites ($11.39). The Bucket Feast (about $24.78) is built for groups.

Breakfast on the Sheetz menu

Breakfast is served 24/7 at most stores. Burritos start at about $3.19, the Shmuffin is about $3.99, Shmagels and Waffle Sandwiches about $4.59, and two Classic Breakfast Burritos are $6 with the 2/$6 deal.

Coffee, shakes and smoothies

A medium (16 oz) customizable coffee is about $1.99, one of the cheapest coffees on the road. An Iced Latte is about $3.49, and seasonal lattes like the Cinnamon Roll or Sh'morez Hot Latte about $4.19–$4.29. Cheezecake milkshakes run about $5.49–$5.79 and the Firewerkz Shake about $6.29. Smoothies range from about $4.59 (Sh'trawberry Fantasy) to $6.99 (Pineapple Aloha and Dazzling Dragon).

Lemonades, Refresherz and specialty drinkz

Build Your Own Lemonade starts at about $3.49, with flavoured lemonades like Berry, Black Raspberry and Dragon Fruit around $4.49. Refresherz, including ones with mango popping bubblez, are about $4.59. Specialty drinks such as '76 Pop ($4.29) and the Red, White & Berry Sh'parkler ($4.99) come and go with the seasons. The full list of sodas, energy drinks, teas and frozen drinks is on the Sheetz Drink Menu page.

Fan-favourite MTO hacks

Because the Sheetz menu is so customisable, fans share their own builds online, such as a Mac and Cheese Burger, a Buffalo Mac Wrap, a Fry-Stuffed Chicken Sandwich, Boom Boom Loaded Fryz and an Espresso Shake. These aren't official items, but you can usually build them on the touchscreen. The price depends on what you add.

Saving money at Sheetz

  • Join Sheetz Rewardz to earn points on food, drinks and fuel.
  • Watch the app for weekly deals and bundle prices.
  • Choose a medium coffee at about $1.99 instead of a specialty latte.
  • Share a big side: one order of Fryz or Mozzarella Sticks feeds two or three.

Why Sheetz prices vary

Sheetz has stores across Pennsylvania, Ohio, Virginia, West Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina and Michigan, and prices can change by state and store. The prices on this page are typical prices, last checked in November 2026. The Sheetz app shows the exact price for your store.

Sheetz menu FAQ

What is on the Sheetz menu?

The Sheetz menu includes made-to-order burgers, subs, chicken sandwiches, wraps and bowls, salads, Fryz and other sides, all-day breakfast, coffee and lattes, cheezecake milkshakes, smoothies, lemonades, Refresherz, bakery sweets and combo bundles.

How much is food at Sheetz?

Most Sheetz menu items cost between about $2 and $11. A Classic Burger is about $3.99, a 6-inch Meatball Sub about $4.99, an MTO chicken sandwich about $4.99 and a Made To Order Salad about $8.49. Prices vary by state and store.

What does MTO mean at Sheetz?

MTO stands for Made To Order. You build your food on the in-store touchscreen or in the Sheetz app, choosing the base, protein, cheese, toppings and sauce, and it is made fresh for you.

Is the Sheetz menu available 24 hours?

Yes, most Sheetz stores are open 24/7 and serve the MTO menu, including breakfast, around the clock. Hours can differ at a few locations.

What is the cheapest item on the Sheetz menu?

One of the cheapest items is the Chocolate Chip Cookie at about $0.99. A medium customizable coffee is about $1.99 and the Shnack Wrap about $2.49.

Does Sheetz have a secret menu?

Not officially, but because the MTO menu is so customisable, fans build items like a Mac and Cheese Burger, Buffalo Mac Wrap or Boom Boom Loaded Fryz on the touchscreen. The price depends on the add-ons you choose.

Does Sheetz have a kids menu?

Sheetz does not have an official kids menu, but smaller items like the Shnack Wrap, Mac 'n Cheese Bites, cookies and a Nesquik Chocolate Pint work well for children.

Are Sheetz menu prices the same everywhere?

No. Sheetz prices can change by state and store. The prices on this page are typical prices; the Sheetz app shows the exact price at your local store.
