A BETTER STOP STARTS HERE

BEFORE YOU
MAKE THE CALL.

A few small checks help you turn an interesting menu into an order that fits your day.

THE PRICE CHECK

Compare the whole order.

A main item on its own and a full meal are different purchases. Include the side, drink and changes before comparing totals.

Use the final checkout amount for your budget. A reference price gives you context, while the selected location supplies the actual amount.

THE PORTION CHECK

Look beyond the photo.

Read the item description and serving size. A photograph helps you recognize the food, but it does not explain every available portion.

When sharing, list what each person wants first. That makes it easier to spot duplicate extras and missing drinks.

THE PICKUP CHECK

Choose the right stop.

Confirm the restaurant address and collection method together. Review the time shown for your selected order before you finish.

If you change locations, check the menu again. Your preferred product or customization may differ from one ordering screen to another.

ONE LAST THING

QUICK
ANSWERS.

What does MTO mean?+

MTO means Made-To-Order. Browse the customization choices offered for your item at the location you select.

Are these the final restaurant prices?+

No. These are dated menu reference examples. Confirm the portion, extras and final total in the ordering service before paying.

Can I place an order on this page?+

This is an independent guide. It helps you explore the menu but does not accept payment or send food orders.

How do I compare drink options?+

Start with the drink type and size. Then compare the flavor and any additions shown for the exact cup you want.

Does changing an item change its calories?+

The figures shown describe the reference listing. Review the nutrition details for your selected ingredients and portion when customizing.
